## You are invited

We are opening the school to visiting families on **[DATE], from [TIME] to [TIME]**.

Most school visits happen on a quiet Saturday, in an empty building. Ours does not. We hold our open mornings **while the classrooms are running**, because a room full of furniture tells you nothing and a room full of working children tells you everything.

## What you will see

- The **three-hour Montessori work cycle** actually happening — children choosing their own work and staying with it
- The **prepared environment** across all five areas, from Practical Life to Mathematics
- **Our guides working with individual children**, which is the part that is hardest to describe and easiest to recognise
- The **garden and outdoor space**, and how it is used every day
- Where the children **eat, wash and rest**

## Who will be there

- **[NAME]**, Head of School — admissions, fees and the year ahead
- **[NAME]**, Lead Guide, Montessori 2–5
- **[NAME]**, Playgroup Lead — settling-in for the under-twos
- Current parents, who you are welcome to ask anything at all

## Bring your child

Please do. We would rather see how your child responds to the room than describe it to you. There will be materials out for visiting children to try.

## What to ask us

Bring hard questions. Ours to start you off:

- What is your ratio, room by room?
- How long have your teachers been here?
- What is the total annual cost, including everything?
- What happens when a child is having a difficult time?

We have written a longer version of this list — [How to Choose a Preschool in Colombo](/news/how-to-choose-a-preschool-in-colombo) — and we are entirely comfortable with you using it on us.
