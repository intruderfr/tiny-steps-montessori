## Built for how working parents in Colombo actually live

If you work in Colombo 1 to 3 and live around Dehiwala, a 12:30 PM pickup is not a schedule — it is a problem you solve five times a week.

Our daycare extends the day to 5:00 PM in the same building, with the same faces your child already knows. There is no transfer, no second set of adults to get used to, no van.

## What the afternoon looks like

The morning is Montessori work. The afternoon is deliberately not more of that. Children who have concentrated hard all morning need rest, food and unstructured time — not a second shift.

| Time | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| 12:30 | Hot lunch, eaten together at low tables |
| 1:15 | Quiet time — books, then lights down |
| 1:45 | Nap on mats for those who sleep; quiet corner for those who do not |
| 3:00 | Wake slowly, snack |
| 3:30 | Garden — climbing, water, sand, running |
| 4:15 | Homework hour for school-age children, free play for the rest |
| 5:00 | Home |

## The nap is real

We mention this because parents ask, and because the answer varies enormously between schools. There are proper mats, the room is darkened, and a caregiver stays in the room the entire time.

Children who no longer nap are never made to lie there for two hours. They get a quiet activity in the adjacent space — puzzles, books, drawing.

## Homework hour

For our 5–8 year olds coming from primary school, 4:15 to 5:00 is a quiet, supervised stretch with an adult available to help.

We help children work out how to approach a problem. We do not do the work for them, and we will tell you honestly if something is being consistently sent home that your child cannot yet do independently.

## Flexible bookings

Not every family needs five days.

- **Full-time** — Monday to Friday
- **Part-time** — any two or three fixed days
- **Occasional days** — booked ahead when you need them
- **Emergency cover** — call us; if we have space that day, we will take your child

Occasional and emergency care depends on availability and is charged per day. Ring rather than email if it is same-day.

> Food is freshly cooked vegetarian and served at the school. Tell us about allergies, intolerances and anything your family does not eat, in writing, before the first day.
